Manifesto

My parents are farmers.

I grew up knowing how hard it can be to earn a living in the Philippines. As I grew older, I made myself a promise: I would find a way to help build a better tomorrow for Filipinos.

Magasaka grew from that promise. Today, we carry it together.

We want the work Filipinos do for the world to help them build a better life at home.

Filipino talent can compete with the best in the world. Opportunities should reflect people’s ability and the trust they earn through their work. Those beginning their careers need a real chance to prove themselves, too.

Good work deserves fair compensation. Its rewards should stay with the people who earn them, giving them more freedom to care for themselves and build the life they want.

People should have room to plan beyond the next payment. As Magasaka grows, we will work to help talent build savings, understand their tax responsibilities, and care for their health.

Businesses place real trust in the people they hire. They need capable partners who understand their goals and follow through on work that matters to them.

That trust depends on both sides keeping their commitments. We stand for clear expectations, accountability, and respect for each other’s time. Everyone deserves protection in their work and a fair hearing when things go wrong. Privacy and dignity are part of that responsibility.

We believe Filipino talent and businesses can help each other grow. As a business succeeds, the people whose work helped it get there should have room to grow with it.

For me, that responsibility reaches back to the farms.

In the future, we will share a portion of what Magasaka earns to support farmers and the wider Philippine agricultural sector. We will hold ourselves to this promise, alongside our commitments to talent and businesses, as Magasaka grows.

Families like mine are part of why Magasaka exists. I want them to share in what it makes possible.

Sumama ka sa ani.
