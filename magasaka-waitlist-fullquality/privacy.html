Privacy Policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

Magasaka is building a freelance marketplace that connects Filipino talent with clients around the world. This page explains what we collect when you join the waitlist and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • Your first name, your title and up to three skills, if you add them.
  • Basic, anonymous information about how the page is used, such as which device and browser you are on.

How we use it

  • To keep your place on the waitlist and send you your invite when Magasaka opens.
  • To send you occasional updates about the launch.
  • To understand which kinds of talent are joining, so we can bring the right clients.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone except the services that help us run the waitlist (for example, our email provider), who may only use it for that.

Your choices

You can leave the waitlist or ask us to delete your information at any time. Use the unsubscribe link in any of our emails, or reply to one and tell us.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If the change is significant, we will email you.
