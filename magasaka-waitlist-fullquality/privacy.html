Privacy Policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

What we collect when you join the Magasaka waitlist, why, who sees it, and how to have it changed or deleted.

Magasaka is building a freelance marketplace that connects Filipino talent with clients around the world. This policy covers the waitlist website at magasaka.com (the "site"). In it, "Magasaka", "we", "us" and "our" mean the team operating Magasaka, and "you" means anyone who visits the site or joins the waitlist. We handle personal information in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules, and Magasaka is the personal information controller for the information described here.

The waitlist is not the marketplace. When the marketplace opens it will have its own terms and privacy policy, and we will ask for your agreement to them before you use it.

1. What we collect

What you give us when you join.

  • Your email address.
  • Your first name, and the professional title and up to three skills or niches you choose. These appear on your Magasaka card.

What the site creates for your signup.

  • Your waitlist number, a referral code and share link, and, if you joined through someone's link, which member referred you.
  • The dates you joined, completed your profile and (later) confirmed your email.
  • A random key that your browser keeps so it can finish or update your signup. We store only a scrambled (hashed) version of it, which cannot be turned back into the key.

Technical information. To stop spam and abuse, the site briefly counts how many signup attempts come from the same connection. Your IP address is turned into a one-way code mixed with a secret key before it is counted, the raw IP address is not saved, and the counts are deleted after about two minutes.

What stays on your device. The site saves a few small things in your own browser's storage so it works the way you left it: your signup progress and key, whether you turned the farm sounds on or off, how many times the intro has played, where you were on the page, and whether Mang Juan has already greeted you. This stays in your browser and is not sent to us, except the signup details above when you submit them. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Mang Juan. Right now, Mang Juan's answers come from a fixed set of replies inside the page, and what you type to him is not sent to us or saved. If we later connect him to an AI service so he can answer more freely, we will update this policy first and say so in the chat.

What we do not collect. No passwords, no payment details, no government IDs, no precise location, and no camera or microphone access. The site does not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics.

2. How we use it

  • To hold your place on the waitlist, show your card and keep your referral link working.
  • To email you about your waitlist spot, to ask you to confirm your email before launch, and to send your invite when it is ready.
  • To send occasional updates about the launch. Every update has a way to stop them.
  • To understand, in aggregate, which kinds of talent are joining (for example, how many video editors or bookkeepers), so we can bring the right clients and plan the launch.
  • To keep the site secure and working, and to prevent spam, fraud and abuse.
  • To meet our legal obligations and protect our rights and the rights of others.

We process this information because you asked to join the waitlist and it is needed to run it (our legitimate interest), and on your consent where the law requires it. We do not use your information to make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you, and we do not sell it.

3. Who we share it with

  • Service providers who help us run the waitlist, such as our website hosting and database provider and, once we start sending email, our email delivery provider. They may use your information only to provide their service to us, under agreements that require them to protect it.
  • The person who referred you. If we later show members how many people joined through their link, we will show only a count or, at most, your first name, never your email address.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or to protect the safety, rights or property of our members, the public or Magasaka.
  • A future owner of Magasaka, if the business is merged, acquired or reorganized, who must keep handling your information in line with this policy.

We will not share your waitlist information with clients or employers. When the marketplace opens, your profile will be shown to clients only after you create your marketplace account and choose to.

4. Where it is stored

Our hosting and service providers may store and process information on servers outside the Philippines. When they do, we take reasonable steps, including contracts, to make sure your information receives a level of protection comparable to Philippine law.

5. How long we keep it

  • Waitlist information is kept until the marketplace launches and you have had the chance to create an account, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first.
  • If you do not create an account within 12 months after launch, or if Magasaka does not launch, we will delete your waitlist information or make it anonymous.
  • Anti-spam counts are deleted after about two minutes.
  • We may keep a record that you asked to stop receiving emails, so we can respect it.

6. How we protect it

We use reasonable organizational, physical and technical measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), limited access, hashing of signup keys and connection data, and limits on repeated requests. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but if a breach puts your information at real risk, we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission as the law requires.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your information is used (this policy);
  • access the information we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate or incomplete information corrected;
  • object to processing, and withdraw your consent, including to updates;
  • have your information deleted or blocked;
  • receive your information in a common electronic format (data portability);
  • be compensated for damages caused by inaccurate, incomplete or unlawfully used information; and
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

To use any of these rights, email us at the address below from the email you joined with. We may ask a simple question to confirm it is you, and we will reply within the time the law allows. Leaving the waitlist is free.

8. Age

The waitlist is for people who are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe someone under 18 has joined, contact us and we will delete their information.

9. Links to other sites

The site links to our pages on Facebook, X and LinkedIn, and lets you share your link there. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which apply once you go to them.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If the change is significant, for example a new use of your information, we will email you before it takes effect and, where the law requires, ask for your consent.

11. Contact us

For questions, requests or complaints about your information, email privacy@magasaka.com. This address reaches the person responsible for privacy at Magasaka (our Data Protection Officer).
